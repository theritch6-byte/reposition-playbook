STR Market Analysis · August 21, 2026

Kachina Village

Arizona · Unincorporated Coconino County
Kachina Village is a genuinely excellent place to operate a short-term rental and a poor place to buy one at $500,000. The regulatory framework is close to best-in-class for an investor — unincorporated Coconino County, not the City of Flagstaff — and the market posts the best occupancy in the I-17 south corridor on five durable demand drivers. But a 2BR grosses about $38,000 while asking $440,000–$620,000: a 5.74%–7.6% gross yield and a 2.0%–2.8% cap rate against a 7.75% note, which is deep structural negative leverage. At 5.90/10, Kachina Village ranks below the mountain-market median among STR markets — well below anchors like Banner Elk / Shenandoah Valley (7.6–8.4) and below Luray, VA (6.00, Tier D — run Aug 23 2026), sitting instead near the single-driver COVID-boom band (5.4–6.2) despite having genuinely durable demand. All three buy-box lanes fail your 20% revenue floor by more than 2× and every bear case is Distress. Do not build a buy box here at the $500,000 cap.
Budget Fit — TIGHT
At $465,000, reaching $22,000 net cash flow requires roughly $79,178 in gross revenue — 65% above this market’s $48,000 ceiling for a 3BR. Under true remote-owner costs (the analysis modelled an out-of-state owner, so a paid local designee was included) the requirement rises to $97,194, more than double the ceiling. No Kachina Village property under the $500,000 cap reaches either figure.
⚠ Jurisdiction Warning
Kachina Village is unincorporated Coconino County, governed by County Ordinance 2023-04 — not the City of Flagstaff, whose STR rules are the most restrictive in the region. The trap is that Kachina’s mailing address reads “Flagstaff, AZ 86005.” Confirm every parcel against the Coconino County parcel viewer or Community Development (928-679-8856) before making any offer — buying inside city limits by mistake changes the rules, the 6.90% lodging tax (to ≈11.39%), and the entire thesis.

⚖ Regulatory & Legal
Jurisdiction — CONFIRMED

Unincorporated Coconino County, ZIP 86005. Never incorporated, no annexation found. Governed by County Ordinance 2023-04 under A.R.S. §11-269.17 — NOT the City of Flagstaff. Zoning is predominantly RS-6,000 / RS-10,000, pulled live from county GIS.

Permit

$250/yr, 7-business-day issuance, no inspection, no cap, no owner-occupancy requirement, no minimum stay, no neighbor approval, no lottery.

Emergency Contact — NOT a blocker

Statute says “owner or the owner’s designee” with no distance rule anywhere. A paid Flagstaff-area cleaner or handyman qualifies. County practice: 24-hour contact, 72-hour non-emergency response. Budget $2,400/yr.

Lodging Tax Advantage

6.90% in unincorporated Coconino vs. ≈11.39% inside Flagstaff — about 4.5 points of guest-side price headroom. Airbnb and Vrbo auto-remit in Arizona, but the owner must still file returns using Deduction Code 775, including $0 returns.

Property Tax — Legal Class 4

Investor STRs are Class 4, which forfeits the state-aid-to-education credit ($370–$378/yr verified on live bills). Prop 117 means LPV does NOT reset to purchase price on sale, and §42-13302(A)(2)(a) confirms verbatim that the Class 3→4 flip is not a change in use — so Rule B does not trigger. Effective rate 0.65% of purchase price, tax area 0104 (SD#1 FD Highlands).

Habitable-Dwelling Screen

STRs are limited to lawfully permitted single-family dwellings. Garages, sheds, tents, travel trailers, RVs and temporary structures are explicitly excluded. Domes, yurts, park models and skoolies do NOT qualify — no variance path.

HOA / CC&R — UNRESOLVED

The one item that needs your attention. The county’s Kachina Village Area Plan states the original HOA disbanded and covenants went unenforced — but disbanded is not released, a nearby sub-association exists (Kachina Parkway HOA), and later subdivision units can carry live CC&Rs. The county issues the permit regardless; the permit is not a legal opinion. Clear this with parcel-specific title work as a written go/no-go contingency.

Comparison Set

Kachina Village, Munds Park, Mountainaire and Fort Valley are regulatorily identical — same ordinance, fee, tax and enforcement body. The only regulatory decision in this region is inside-Flagstaff versus outside. One operational differentiator: Kachina is on KVID community sewer while Mountainaire is largely on-lot septic.

📈 Demand & Market Fundamentals
Five Verified Demand Drivers

Phoenix heat-escape (dominant), Grand Canyon South Rim ~1h30, Sedona / Oak Creek ~45 min, Arizona Snowbowl ~35 min, and Flagstaff itself (NAU, Lowell Observatory, Route 66). No fewer-than-three flag.

Phoenix Heat-Escape — quantified

Phoenix metro 4.83M, 2h05–2h15 on I-17, 20–25°F cooler typically and 30°F+ on peak days. Booking-origin data confirms it: Phoenix 16.13% + Scottsdale 4.15% + Tucson 5.37% ≈ 26% of all Flagstaff bookings.

Weekend Compression — model it

A 2h15 drive is Friday-evening distance. A 53% blended occupancy with a strong weekend means midweek runs in the 30s. Any flat-occupancy pro forma overstates revenue.

Wildfire is a DEMAND risk, not just a cost

A materially demand-suppressing fire or closure in roughly 6 of the last 9 summers. Full Coconino NF closure June 23–July 6, 2021. The 2026 Pocket Fire (27,393 acres) closed SR-89A for 25 days between Fort Tuthill and north Sedona — immediately adjacent to Kachina Village, which was placed in SET evacuation status. All five drivers route through one physical asset, so they fail together and at peak. Budget a 2–4 peak-week vacancy reserve (~4–7% of gross).

Revenue Trend

Flat to softening. AirDNA May 2025→May 2026: revenue +0.4%, occupancy −4.1%, ADR −0.7%, RevPAR −7.3%. Airbtics grades Flagstaff “C — lowest 20% for STR yield nationally.” Not flagged as formally DECLINING because 2+ consecutive declining periods could not be confirmed.

Vendor Disagreement — material

AirDNA and Airbtics disagree violently on both listings (−13.6% vs. +16.2%) and revenue (+0.4% vs. +25.2%). PriceLabs Market Research — the one tool that could have resolved this at ZIP level — is not enabled on this account. Treat every single-vendor number as directional.

Forward Signals

I-17 widening and flex lanes substantially complete 2025, directly attacking the Sunday Phoenix-return bottleneck. Snowbowl $2.5M capex on snowmaking. NAU enrollment +2%. Lowell’s new Astronomy Discovery Center.

Negative Signal

Northern Arizona Healthcare paused its new Flagstaff Medical Center in February 2026, killing a construction-crew demand pulse and ~315 planned housing units.

Kachina-Specific Shoulder Play

The village sits in elk Game Management Unit 6A (Mormon Lake). September archery rut and an 825-permit late rifle hunt Nov 28–Dec 4 land squarely in the two dead shoulders (April–May mud season; mid-September to early November).

🏠 Competitive & Revenue
THE CENTRAL FINDING

A Kachina 2BR grosses ~$38,000/yr (band $34K–$42K) while 2BR/2BA site-builts ask $440,000–$620,000. Best-fit price is $126,667 at the 30% goal and $190,000 at the 20% floor — a 2.3× to 5× gap. At $500,000 the revenue-to-price ratio is 7.6%, about one-third of the floor.

Even the best property fails

The top 10% of Kachina listings gross $90,960/yr; at $500,000 that is 18.2% — still under the 20% floor. And those top-decile properties are 4–6BR homes trading at $700K–$1M. The #1 earner is a 6BR at $191,997.

Small Market Flag — TRIGGERED

130–136 active Kachina Airbnb listings. Revenue benchmarks carry higher variance; treat medians as directional. Supply & Competition confidence is capped at Medium as a result. Greater Flagstaff (1,016–2,741 listings) is NOT a small market and supplies the bedroom-level shape.

Kachina wins its corridor

Best occupancy (46.3%) and best median gross ($39,504) of the I-17 south corridor — beating Flagstaff city, Munds Park and Mountainaire — despite a lower ADR. Superhost rate 76.9%, highest of the sub-areas. Kachina fills its calendar; it is the acquisition price that fails.

Sub-Area Comparison

Kachina Village $269 ADR / 46.3% / $39,504 median • Flagstaff city $303 / 42.4% / $37,668 • Munds Park $311 / 35.7% / $35,196 • Mountainaire $232 / 40.8% / $30,624 (shedding supply at −23.4%) • Fort Valley $422 / 42.5% / $53,760 (but median asking $2,275,000 — out of reach).

Bedroom Bands (Flagstaff, 2 sources)

1BR $22,500–$28,142 • 2BR $32,580–$38,261 • 3BR $40,200–$47,580 • 4BR $52,773–$59,050. Re-indexed to Kachina at 0.97× the ADR curve. Two-source rule satisfied at the Flagstaff bedroom level (Rabbu + Chalet); the Kachina 2BR band itself is single-source direct and tagged Low–Moderate.

Sweet spot is 4–5BR, not 2BR

Revenue roughly doubles from 2BR to 4BR while Kachina asking prices barely move. But the thesis fails on price: site-built 4BR+ asks $725,000 and sold $580K–$863K over 12 months. Only one traded under $500K.

Amenity Gaps — real opportunity

Hot tub penetration only 25% (and just 1 of 14 active listings has one), EV charging 9%, pet-friendly 38%. A/C is confirmed unnecessary at 7,000 ft (July average high 82°F). MTR share is 38.5%, just under the 40% adjustment trigger but real — NAU plus travel-nurse demand gives midweek a floor.

Revenue Floor — SPLIT

Threshold $2,006/mo (70% of monthly debt service). Kachina market-average February $2,902 PASSES; 2BR indexed off AirROI $2,622 PASSES; 2BR indexed off Rabbu’s harsher read $1,790 FAILS. Both Kachina-anchored bases clear, but a 2BR has no dead-month cushion.

🔧 Operations & Exit
Manufactured Screen — READ THIS BEFORE ANY OFFER

36% of active Kachina inventory is manufactured, concentrated exactly in the buy zone, and two listings actively obscure it. 2449 Blue Gap Ovi ($450,000) is marketed as “Affordable Newer Construction… durable 2x6 construction” and is manufactured (2020). 3206 Mesa Trl ($465,000), the only 4BR under $500K, is manufactured (2019). Strip manufactured out and the site-built 3–4BR median jumps from $465,000 to $602,500. Verify on the assessor record, not the listing copy.

Elevation OpEx — $12,500, not $4,000

Itemized across 12 lines: metered KVID water/sewer ($1,900 on the district’s own published tier schedule), snow plowing ($1,100 — the county plows public roads only, never driveways), propane/heating, freeze protection, insurer-mandated defensible-space clearing ($600), higher electric, and the $250 county STR registration. The standard $4,000 baseline understates this market by more than 3×. Premise correction: UniSource natural gas serves Kachina Village, so it is not propane-only.

Insurance — no FAIR plan in Arizona

A declined parcel has no state backstop: surplus lines or uninsured. State Farm has halted new property applications in Arizona’s high country. Bands LOW $4,200 / MID $6,500 / HIGH $11,000, plus $800 standalone NFIP flood. Recommended pro forma figure $7,300. Kachina Village was in SET evacuation status during the June 2026 Pocket Fire. Treat E&S as the practical primary market.

Flood — check the parcel

Kachina is NOT in the Museum or Pipeline post-fire flood watersheds (those are Spruce Wash and Rio de Flag, north and east of the city). But the 2022 Pipeline Fire feeds Pumphouse Wash — literally the assessor’s land code on Old Munds Hwy parcels — and the county’s 2022/2025 remap left some Kachina parcels in Zone A/AE. Zone A/AE flood runs $1,500–$3,000+ vs. $400–$800 preferred. Get an elevation certificate before releasing the inspection contingency.

Local Contact — priced, not a blocker

$2,400/yr ($200/mo retainer). Cheaper than the Williams run’s $4,200 because the responder pool is Flagstaff at 12 minutes, not 35 miles. Flagstaff Cleaning Company, Pine Country Windows and the Turno marketplace all serve the area.

Cleaning — the one line that behaves

Roughly revenue-neutral: ~60 turns × ~$115 cost = $6,900 against a Flagstaff median guest cleaning fee of $160 charged by 92.4% of listings = ~$9,600 collected. Caveat: a $160 fee against a ~$270 ADR makes a 2-night stay 23% cleaning, suppressing short shoulder bookings.

Land-to-Building — 0.63, worse than Williams

Coconino assigns a flat $145,445 land value to every standard Kachina lot, so vintage drives the split: 0.73 newer (2000+) / 0.62 mid-age / 0.61 pre-1980 / 0.20 Kachina Mobiles. From 68 real EagleWeb parcels, TY2026. The same dollar of purchase price buys ~21% less depreciable basis here than in Williams (0.63 vs. 0.80). Cost seg is still worth running: ~5–9× return in year one against a $3,000–$4,000 study fee.

Appreciation — mixed, currently negative

FHFA Flagstaff MSA: +8.6%/yr over 5 years but only +3.9%/yr over 3, peaking at 495.64 in 2025Q4 then falling to 475.19 in 2026Q1 (−4.1% in a quarter). Zillow has Flagstaff at ~$665,540, −0.5% YoY — a real-terms loss. Meanwhile LPV compounds at exactly the 5.00% Prop-117 cap, so taxes rise faster than values.

LTR Exit — tested honestly, and it FAILS

Flagstaff 2BR apartments run $2,100–$2,317/mo with vacancy under 7%; a Kachina 2BR realistically rents $1,800–$2,400. At $2,100/mo, NOI ≈ $13,870 = a 2.8% cap against $34,388 of annual P&I — leaving the owner ~$20,500/yr short. A strong occupancy backstop; not a debt-service backstop.

Resale

Median DOM in Kachina ~98 days (range 12–168), with three of the most interesting listings at 120/142/150 days and two already price-cut. That is real negotiating room — and a signal that the market is currently a buyer’s market.
