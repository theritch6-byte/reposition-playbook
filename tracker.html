🎯 What we're aiming for

Guides, not gates — we can trade a few. (And ignore AirDNA's market rank/score — it's meaningless.)

🌟 North star: capture something nature made that can't be replicated — a river, red rock, canyon rim, lake, or old-growth trees — in a unique house, close to something fun, at our price. A competitor can copy a hot tub; they can't copy what God made.

📖 What each column means

ColumnWhat it is / how it's figured
Median rev/yrAirDNA's median annual revenue for STRs in the sub-market (the middle listing's yearly income). The % next to it is the year-over-year (YoY) change — up or down vs. last year.
InvestabilityAirDNA's 0–100 score for how good the market is to invest in (blends revenue, growth, regulation, etc.). Your target ~80. NOT the same as the "rank," which we ignore.
ADRAverage Daily Rate — the average nightly price. Tracked only, no target.
ListingsNumber of active STR listings in the sub-market (market size + competition). The % is vs. last year. Your floor: 100+.
ROI @ $500KGross revenue ÷ $500,000 = the gross yield. Your 30% goal / 20% floor test at a $500K price. Uses purchase price alone — renovation NOT included.
Rev/yr (Rentalizer)AirDNA's projected annual revenue for one specific address.
Rev % of priceThat property's Rev/yr ÷ its price = its gross yield (your 30%/20% test).
Moat / featureDoes it capture the irreplaceable natural feature (river, red rock, trees, a one-of-a-kind view) + a unique exterior?
Comp reviewsReviews on the comparable listings — need 25+ to trust the numbers.
VerdictPass or consider — scored against your criteria only.
