STR Market Analysis · August 23, 2026

Blue Ridge

Fannin County · North Georgia Mountains
Blue Ridge has the strongest demand engine and the friendliest regulatory regime of the six markets screened — and none of it is buyable at the cap. Gate Zero verified a median 8.58× price-to-disclosed-revenue with a completely empty left tail (zero listings at or under 6× at any price — no Broken Bow–style exception exists), supply has compounded +64–77% over 3–5 years while occupancy eroded from 68.6% to ~53%, and the $90–115K operators that do clear debt trade at $740K–$925K. At 5.99/10, Blue Ridge ranks in the marginal band among mountain-cabin STR markets — below the ~7.0 Luray calibration anchor and Destin's 6.9–7.5 Tier C band, slotting between Broken Bow (5.90) and Luray (6.00) among the six full runs. The revenue is real (AirDNA reads 11–53% LOW here); the entry price is the failure.
Budget Fit — MARGINAL
At $500K your $22,000 net-CF target needs ~$77,400 gross — top-quartile 3BR execution; the 3BR market median ($55–59K) misses it by ~$20K/yr. If PM-managed (25%), the requirement rises to ~$102,500.
⚠ Jurisdiction Warning
Buy UNINCORPORATED FANNIN only, verified by qPublic parcel — the City of Blue Ridge limits new STR certificates to the CBD and grandfathering dies at sale; Gilmer County (Ellijay, west Cherry Log) host licenses are NON-transferable; Cherry Log 30522 parcels straddle the county line. Fannin's certificate transfers with a 30-day grace + $50 fee.

⚖ Regulatory & Legal
Unincorporated Fannin — The Buy Box

No countywide zoning; STR effectively by-right with a $225/yr Accommodation Excise Tax Certificate (expires Dec 31). Ordinance revised Aug 12, 2025 — no pending amendments or moratoriums found vs the 1031 calendar (ID 09/28/26 · close 02/10/27). Best transfer regime screened: buyer files within 7 days of closing → 30-day operating grace, $50 fee.

City of Blue Ridge + Gilmer County — Excluded

City: new certificates CBD-only; grandfathering dies at sale — in-city cabins (e.g. 266 Ada St) are dead ends for a buyer. Gilmer (Ellijay, west Cherry Log): host license NON-transferable + third-party safety inspection — expect a licensing gap buying any Gilmer-side operating STR. Resolve Cherry Log BY PARCEL on qPublic, never mailing address.

TVA Lake Blue Ridge

Most "lakefront" is TVA takeline / National Forest, not deeded frontage. Section 26a dock permits do NOT auto-transfer (reapply ~60 days post-close) and can be denied — never pay dock pricing without written 26a status.

Tax Stack

7% sales + 6% county excise + $5/night GA hotel-motel fee (~13% + $5). Owner files a monthly county excise report by the 20th even on platform bookings.

Private Gates

With no zoning, recorded covenants are the ONLY private gate — parts of Aska, Coosawattee (Gilmer), and some Old Toccoa Farm tracts restrict STRs. Order covenants with title on EVERY parcel; Sunrise Valley's covenant posture is unverified ($31/mo HOA confirmed). Shared wells (Sunrise Valley, Rocky Top) need a RECORDED well-sharing agreement or lenders balk. GA septic: 150 gpd/bedroom → 2BR = 4 guests, 3BR = 6 — loft-sleeper listings are over-permit (same trap as Luray).

📈 Demand & Market Fundamentals
Deepest Driver Stack Screened

6+ independent drivers: downtown Depot St district, the verified-operating Blue Ridge Scenic Railway (expanded 2026–27 calendar), Toccoa trout/tubing ("Trout Fishing Capital of Georgia"), Aska / Chattahoochee NF trails, Lake Blue Ridge, Mercier Orchards, wineries + leaf season. Visitor spend $231M (2022) → $254M (2024); $7.07M lodging-tax receipts FY2024.

Strongest Feeder Verified

Metro Atlanta (~6.4M) at ~1h35m — wealthy north suburbs ~1h05–1h25 — on four-lane GA-515 the whole way. ~7M within 2h, ~9.5M within 3h. Beats Broken Bow (DFW 3h) and Luray (substitute-rich DC corridor).

Mature Market

Secondary, MATURE/late-majority — an early pre-Airbnb STR market. No early-adopter upside; wide performance spread between designed cabins and commodity cabins. The 2023 correction happened and was digested, but supply is still being absorbed (see Competitive).

Durability

No single point of failure — losing the railway ≈ under 10% of room-nights. Recession trade-down beneficiary (drive-to substitution). ~180 wedding venues; retiree/remote-worker in-migration (+10.9% county since 2010). Watch item: occupancy softness, not demand dollars.

🏠 Competitive & Revenue
Supply — The Two-Source Rule Fired Again

AirDNA's +4.5% YoY is the OUTLIER: AirROI +36.0%, Airbtics +21.8%/yr (+64%/3yr), StaySTRA +77% 2021→2026 (839→1,484) — while occupancy eroded 68.6% → ~53% and ADR kept rising: the classic ADR-masked oversupply signature. Still absorbing, not post-correction. Morganton (−9.8%) and Mineral Bluff (−5.2%) are already in per-listing revenue decline.

Gate Zero — 8.58× and an Empty Left Tail

8 disclosed-actual revenue listings across all 7 ZIPs: clean median 8.58× ask ÷ gross (every sensitivity cut 8.3–9.4×). Zero listings ≤6× at any price; lowest under $600K is 266 Ada St at 8.56×. The 20% floor (5.0×) is arithmetically unreachable — every disclosed listing would need a 40%+ cut.

Calibration — AirDNA Reads LOW Here

Rentalizer vs disclosed actuals: −11% to −53% on 6 real operators (184 High Meadows: $54.1K projected vs $114K documented 3-yr actual). Comp tables corroborate $95–109K actual trailing revenue on 3–4BR. Inverts Luray; matches Broken Bow. No seller-haircut view applies — the fail stands at face value.

Bedroom Ladder (two-sourced)

2BR: $41.6–44.1K median / ~$75K 90th pct — the 2BR guide misses the $100K floor even at the 90th percentile. 3BR: $55.3–58.7K / ~$91K 90th. 4BR: ~$73K / $103.9K top-quartile / $123.4K top-decile — the only count whose top-quartile clears $100K. 54.3% of the market sleeps 8+; this is a 4BR+ group market.

No Accessible Moat

Treehouses run 1.04–1.14× market ADR (FAIL vs the 2× bar; Broken Bow's ran 5×). A-frames UNDERPERFORM log cabins ($38.5K vs $44.8K). Riverfront is a peak-amplifier on 4BR+, not a 2× moat at 2–3BR. Hot tubs are 82.4%-prevalence table stakes. Only true scarcities: Lake Blue Ridge deeded frontage (+28%, n=3) and pools (+215% uplift, 1.9% prevalence) — neither buyable at the cap. Walkable-downtown shows no out-earning evidence.

Geofence Order + Floor Test

Shop Blue Ridge proper FIRST, Epworth/Fightingtown Creek SECOND (county-best ADR $438, only rising sub-area, +6%), Cherry Log THIRD. Avoid Morganton/Mineral Bluff medians; Aska carries a −19% earnings discount. Revenue floor: median worst month Feb $3,384 = 118% of full P&I — PASS 1.69×; bottom quartile fails by −5%. ALOS 3.4 → plan 45–55 turns/yr.

🔧 Operations & Exit
Cheapest Tax Bill of 6 Markets

propTax ≈ $2,900/yr on a $500K basis (40% × ~14.3 unincorporated mills ≈ 0.57%). Assessor chases sales — 48 Lookout was assessed at 99.7% of its 2023 sale — and HB 581 caps homesteads only: investors fully exposed. CUVA rollback flag on 10+ acre parcels.

Best Cost-Seg Profile Screened

buildingPct 86–96% (6 live Fannin qPublic parcels; model 88–90%) → ~$34–43K Year-1 bonus-depreciation shelter on $500K. Every comp parcel pulled is absentee-owned (FL/TN/OH/metro-GA) — a fully financialized market.

Operating Costs

insurance $2,000–4,500 (STR-endorsed; flat inland wind/hail deductibles; the real trap is freeze-vacancy exclusions — keep heat ≥55°F + smart shutoff). miscSupplies $12,800 MID ($9.7K–$18.0K from real local vendors) — never $4,000. Fiber (ETC ~55%) + cheap co-op power ($0.123/kWh) help; hot tubs, gravel drives, freeze exposure hurt.

PM Market

Real range 18–30%, model 25% (Chady publishes 18–25%; nationals 25–35%). Cleaning $150–225/turn from a deep independent bench. Remote self-management from Florida is the market norm here. Expense ratios at reference revenue: selfmanage 35.6% · remote 38.8% · with_pm 60.6% — PM-run loses ~$11.8K/yr at $500K.

Exit Reality

Sale-to-list 92.9–94.5% + DOM 61–138 days = the strongest buyer-side negotiation posture screened (a 7–8% discount is market-normal) — but values are flat-to-down (ZHVI −2.8% YoY) and LTR does NOT cover debt ($2,450 rent vs $2,866 P&I). Resale is the only exit.
