Brevard

Transylvania County · Land of Waterfalls, North Carolina
At 7.11/10, Brevard is the first of seven screened markets to escape Tier D — an upper-tier Selective mountain market above the ~7.0 Luray anchor and clearly above Hocking Hills (6.21), Blue Ridge (5.99), and Broken Bow (5.90). It is the only screened market whose three core fundamentals all point the right way at once: revenue GROWING (three sources), supply SHRINKING (two sources) with occupancy and ADR rising, and a fully by-right county backed by North Carolina's statutory shield. What holds it out of Tier B: the lowest median revenue base of the set (~$33K — the median-priced asset is uninvestable), ZERO disclosed revenue actuals county-wide (T-12 mandatory on every candidate), a borderline-small 536-listing market, and a mild price correction. Workable only on the buy-cheap tail ($231–350K entries or a water-feature parcel) with top-quartile execution — which is exactly the thesis this run was scoped to.
Budget Fit — MARGINAL
At the $360K Target basis your $22,000 net-CF target needs ~$66,600 gross — top-quartile 3BR execution against a 77.9%-static-pricing field. Uniquely among the seven markets screened, the target lands INSIDE the researched band ($62–70K) rather than above it; the 3BR median ($40–45K) leaves ≈ breakeven.

⚖ Regulatory & Legal
Unincorporated Transylvania — Fully By-Right

No county zoning, no STR ordinance, no registration, no permit, no cap, $0 — the friendliest posture of the seven markets screened, and it is STATUTORILY SHIELDED: NC G.S. 160D-1207(c) (upheld in Schroeder v. Wilmington, 2022) bars localities from registration/permit-to-rent schemes. Only zoning-district restriction remains lawful.

City of Brevard + ETJ — Zone-Restricted

Ord. 2023-37 banned NEW whole-home STRs in General Residential; only Mixed-Use / Institutional Campus districts allow them by zoning permit. The city's ETJ ring extends its zoning beyond city limits — jurisdiction must be verified per parcel, never by mailing address. Walkable-downtown candidates must be Mixed-Use-zoned or they are dead ends.

Connestee Falls — STRs BANNED

The county's largest gated POA prohibits <30-day rentals community-wide (grace ended 5/31/2024, explicitly NO grandfathering, $14K amenity fee at closing). Its listings are non-compliant and excluded from all revenue evidence. With no government regulator here, the POA is the real regulator — order recorded CC&Rs + a written association statement on every parcel.

Zoning Clock — Quiet

No pending ordinance, hearing, or moratorium for city or county as of Aug 23, 2026; the city's STR task force disbanded after 2023 rulemaking. The 09/28/26 identify → 02/10/27 close window is regulatorily quiet.

Tax Stack

6.75% sales + 5% county occupancy = 11.75%. Airbnb auto-remits both; Vrbo/direct bookings = owner remits occupancy monthly by the 20th (paper form).

📈 Demand & Market Fundamentals
Five Independent Nature-Anchored Drivers

Pisgah NF (2nd-most-visited national forest in the US), DuPont State Recreational Forest (1.2M+/yr, NC's most-visited — Hunger Games falls), national-caliber mountain biking ("Cycling Capital of the South", 400+ mi of singletrack), Brevard Music Center (90th season, 80+ concerts), October leaf season, plus White Squirrel Weekend, weddings, and Oskar Blues. No single point of failure.

Helene-Stress-Tested

The worst realistic shock already happened: Hurricane Helene (Sept 2024). Transylvania visitor spend gave back only −4.2% in the storm year vs Buncombe/Asheville −10.8%; US-276 reopened in ~2 weeks, DuPont partially in 5; 2019→2024 county visitor spend is +63% INCLUDING the hit. Asheville's loss is measurably Brevard's gain (Buncombe STR revenue −32% H1-2025 while Brevard runs +8.9%).

Feeder — Honest Read

~1.8M within 90 minutes (Asheville 40–45 min, Greenville–Spartanburg ~1.25 hr) — about a third of Blue Ridge's Atlanta pool — with Charlotte 2.25 hr and Atlanta ~3 hr. Offset by five separate feeders, #1 guest origin Charlotte, and genuine national destination pull. Smaller feeder, stickier demand.

Catalysts Inside the Hold Window

Ecusta Trail rail-trail: first 6 mi opened July 2025, full 19 mi into downtown Brevard funded and due late 2027 ($24.5M building the Brevard end). Plus the $30M Brevard Staytion lodge and the DuPont master plan with state funding. REVENUE_TREND: GROWING, three-source (+8.9% / +9.5% / +6.3%).

🏠 Competitive & Revenue
The Inverse-Oversupply Signature — First of 7

Supply is FLAT-TO-SHRINKING, two-source confirmed (AirDNA −3.8% county submarket; AirROI Pisgah Forest −17.6%, Brevard city held steady) while occupancy (+3.9%) AND ADR (+4.7%) rise. Every prior market showed the opposite. Helene caveat: treat ~1/3 of the contraction as potentially temporary storm delistings.

Gate Zero — A Disclosure Null (N=0)

A full Redfin remarks sweep of all 7 county ZIPs found ZERO listings disclosing actual revenue — nothing to compute a disclosed-multiple median from. Consequence: every revenue figure in this market is a projection or platform estimate, so a written T-12 (or platform export) is MANDATORY before any offer, with the review-math sniff test and a forward-calendar check.

The Waterfall Moat — Verified, First Accessible Moat Screened

Water-on-parcel clears the 2× ADR bar decisively: tracked actuals of $148.3K (3BR backyard waterfall, 83% occ, ADR $665 = 3.0×), $100K (1BR waterfall apt, 81%), $75.8K (3/2 with two private waterfalls on 6 acres); live waterfall listings price $572–650/night (2.6–3.0×). The moat is the WATER, not the structure — Rosman domes price at 0.87× market (dome premium NOT proven), A-frame/treehouse unpriced. Walkable-downtown also earns ($65.3K/77% tracked).

Bands + Small Market

Median $29–37K (four sources; the LOWEST base of the set); top-quartile ≈$51K; top-decile ≈$78K market-wide. 3BR: $40–45K median / $62–70K top-quartile / $95–105K top-decile (scale-reconciled ±15% — no free source publishes Brevard bedroom medians). SMALL MARKET flag TRIGGERS (536 all-platform < 600) — thin comp sets, wider error bars. Dual-platform is standard (Vrbo real: 604 houses + 218 cabins browseable).

The Operator Gap = Revenue Management

Host quality is high (70% Superhost, 4.92★, 68.8% Guest Favorite) but 77.9% run static pricing and only 20% are professionally managed — pricing discipline alone enters the top quartile. Pets-allowed carries a +332% revenue uplift. ALOS 4.7 nights → 30–40 turns/yr. Peaks July AND October (~13–14% each); trough Jan–Feb.

Geofence + Floor

Shop Brevard city → Pisgah Forest (supply −17.6%, revenue +11.4%) → Penrose (40.6% occ, top-10% $93.9K). Cedar Mountain only for a DuPont-corridor feature parcel; Lake Toxaway (−5.9% rev, $365 ADR luxury game) and Rosman (−3.3%) avoid-unless-feature. Revenue floor: at $300K debt the worst median month PASSES both sources (+40% to +103%); at $500K it is SPLIT — the buy-cheap basis is what makes winter survivable.
