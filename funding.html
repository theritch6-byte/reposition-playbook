What "seller covers closing + prepaids" means — and the 6% ceiling: A seller concession is money the seller agrees, in the contract, to put toward your closing costs + prepaids. Parker confirmed a DSCR loan allows up to 6% of price — but 6% is our CEILING, not the expectation; realistically we'd ask only enough to cover closing + prepaids (~2.5–3%). It's taken two ways: (A) seller covers closing + prepaids directly, or (B) a credit up to 6% — any room left under 6% after closing can buy down your rate. You ask for it in the offer; the seller can say yes/no/counter, so it is never guaranteed, and a concession can only pay closing / prepaids / rate-buydown — never cash, never renovation. It simply keeps closing from eating your reno.
