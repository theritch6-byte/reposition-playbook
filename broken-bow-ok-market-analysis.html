STR Market Analysis · August 23, 2026

Broken Bow / Hochatown

Oklahoma · McCurtain County
Broken Bow / Hochatown is the rare market where the demand thesis is fully intact and the investment thesis is still broken. Five durable drivers (Broken Bow Lake, Beavers Bend, Ouachita NF, a year-round trout tailwater, the $238M Choctaw Landing casino) held visitation at 2.0–2.8M/yr — and supply answered louder than demand: ~400 cabins became 3,200–4,500 (+76% in 3 years), with ADR and occupancy falling and asset prices −15–25% off peak. Regulation is genuinely friendly (licensed, no cap, litigation-tested) and seasonality is the flattest of 10 benchmarked mountain markets — but the Gate Zero disclosed-revenue median of 8.5× means a median cabin loses money on 7.75% debt. At 5.90/10, Broken Bow/Hochatown ranks marginal (Tier D) among secondary drive-to cabin STR markets — below Luray, VA (~7.0 Tier C anchor), landing beside Nicole's Hocking Hills (6.21) and Luray (6.00) runs. The one live path: the disclosed-revenue left tail — led by 94 Bent Pine Trl at 4.99×, the only listing in any market screened that passes the 20% floor at asking price.
Budget Fit — TIGHT
At $500K, hitting the $22K net-CF target needs ~$80K gross — above the Target lane’s $70K ceiling. Only the disclosed-revenue left tail (Bent Pine $97.3K, Hygge $80.6K on actuals) reaches it.
⚠ Revenue Trend — Declining
Per-listing revenue is flat-to-declining (Airbtics −5.8% rev, −3.9% occ; 2/2-class ADR −5.5%). AirDNA’s all-listings +3.4% is a luxury-lodge mix-shift artifact. Underwrite to recent actuals and haircut seller peak-year claims 20–30% — though calibration shows disclosed numbers here sit ABOVE AirDNA projections, so those haircuts are conservative floors.
⚠ Jurisdiction Warning
Everything mails as “Broken Bow 74728,” but City of Broken Bow limits confine STRs to C-5/commercial zones — target Town of Hochatown (licensed, no cap) or unincorporated McCurtain County parcels, and verify jurisdiction on the official town-limits map before any offer, never by mailing address.

⚖ Regulatory & Legal
Hochatown — the STR core

Licensed, NO cap, litigation-tested. $300 initial + $100/yr (July 1 renewal; $250/MONTH late fee), self-certification inspection, 24/7 contact required (Ord. 6). STR is a defined principal use in the zoning code (Ord. 18). Incorporation survived trial (2019) and appeal — unwind risk closed. Direction of travel: tax capture, not restriction. Open gap: license transferability on sale.

Broken Bow city limits

Ord. 428 (Feb 2024) bars STRs outside C-5/Commercial-Rec zones, plus a NEW 5% city lodging tax effective 1/1/2026. Avoid city parcels entirely — and everything mails as "Broken Bow," so verify by the town-limits map, never the address.

Tax stack & the Airbnb lawsuit

Hochatown parcel: ≈16.25% guest-paid (9.25% sales + 3% county lodging + 4% town lodging). The town's 4% is HOST-self-remitted via the town portal — Airbnb doesn't reliably remit it (Hochatown has no own ZIP; the town sued Airbnb/Broken Bow/OTC in July 2026 over misrouting). Unincorporated county: ~9.25%, no ordinance, no zoning — friendliest tier.

Choctaw Nation / McGirt

Per Stroble (OK Sup. Ct. 2025, cert denied): no practical effect on a non-tribal buyer of fee-simple land — taxation, permitting, and civil jurisdiction unchanged; the title abstract screens for restricted/trust parcels as routine practice.

POA posture

Most Hochatown subdivisions are purpose-built FOR STRs, but the risk is parcel-level: any listing with an HOA fee or named community is POA-suspect until the recorded CC&Rs + a written association statement clear it. No 1031-window regulatory deadline conflicts found; watch Planning Commission Sep 24 / Oct 29 / Nov 19 / Dec 17, 2026.

📈 Demand & Market Fundamentals
Demand held

Five durable drivers — Broken Bow Lake, Beavers Bend SP, Ouachita NF, a year-round trout tailwater, and the $238M Choctaw Landing casino (Apr 2024, Wyndham-flagged 2025). Visitation ~1M pre-COVID → 2.2M (2021) → holding 2.0–2.8M/yr. Not a faded-boom demand base.

Feeder & access

Pure drive-to: DFW ~3 hrs/175 mi (8M people), ~15M within 4 hrs, no real airport option. Single 2-lane US-259 corridor is the only way in — the access story on any peak weekend.

Lifecycle: late majority

Professionalized PMs (61.9% of listings), price correction underway (−15–25% off peak, 2022-vintage buyers exiting at losses), town incorporation, tax lawsuits, a 20-yr comprehensive plan. The easy-money phase is over; execution wins now.

Year-round claim: partially true

Winter books (trout, casino, holiday events) at materially lower rates — no dead months, but Dec–Feb is a discount season. Demand dips land first on undifferentiated small cabins — exactly the median 2/2 product.

🏠 Competitive & Revenue
Supply — the thesis-killer

Two-sourced: AirROI +29.5% and Airbtics +16.4% YoY (+76% over 3 years); ~400 cabins (2020) → 3,200–4,500 (2026); AirDNA's +0.2% is the outlier. Full oversupply signature: ADR down, occupancy mid-30s%, 450+ cabins for sale at ~101 DOM, building still 20–40% cheaper than buying. Builder starts down >50% — the wave is decelerating but the backlog lands through 2026.

The percentile ladder

Market median ~$50.3K / $373 ADR / 34% occ · top-25% ~$83K · top-10% ~$128.8K / $789 / 67%. The 2BR class averages $35.8K (AirROI)–$45.1K (AirDNA); its top decile ≈ $89–112K. The $100K floor requires top-decile execution; the $150K goal requires an Epic-Treehouse-class outlier ($277K/yr @ 96% occ, $1,926/nt ≈ 5× median ADR — the moat rule passes on structure, not theming).

Gate Zero + calibration

Disclosed-revenue median 8.5× (n=9); left tail: Bent Pine 4.99×, Hygge 6.19×. AirDNA calibration INVERTS Luray: projections read −18% to −45% LOW vs disclosed actuals for strong operators (accurate for a median operator) — board pulls are floors a top operator beats by 1.2–1.6×.

Structure of competition

61.9% professionally managed, 76.3% superhost, 4.86 avg rating; 77% dual-listed (dual-platform = table stakes). Gaps: revenue management (85% on simplified pricing), Instant Book only 25%. ALOS 2.7 nights → 51–91 turns/yr. Geofence: Hochatown first ($63.9–69K avg), Beavers Bend/Lukfata corridor second, Broken Bow city third; Smithville/Idabel/Ringold off the board.

🔧 Operations & Exit
Property tax RESETS on sale

Confirmed on statute (Art. X §8B / 68 O.S. §2817.1) and real parcels: the 5% cap is removed on transfer. McCurtain ratio 11.0%, millage 79.16–83.70 → 0.87–0.92% of price = ~$4,400/yr at $500K. The $1,003–$6,281 dispersion on similar asks is cap-lag — the seller's bill is meaningless; underwrite the reset.

Insurance: hail state, NO safety net

Mid ~$4,000/yr (band $2.8–5.6K) but 1–2% wind/hail percentage deductibles are universal ($5–10K/claim on $500K) and Oklahoma has NO FAIR plan (OK-MAP is referral-only) — a genuine availability tail-risk for a remote-owned STR. ACV roof schedules on older cabins are the hidden cost.

Septic caps revenue

OAC 252:641: 200 gpd ≤2BR + 66/BR after → a standard 2/2 aerobic system legally supports ~6 guests; "sleeps 8" bunk configurations run ~2× design load. Aerobic service contract ~$350/yr, no point-of-sale inspection mandate. Pull the DEQ permit on every finalist.

Cheapest remote lane yet

PM market 15–35% (Stayhocha 15%, SkyRun ~20%) vs Hocking Hills' 40–50%. Ratios at $56K reference: self-manage 0.410 · remote 0.610 · full-PM 0.660. miscSupplies rebuilt from local vendors = $12,900 (LOW $9.8K / HIGH $17.6K) — cheap co-op power and propane offset hot-tub/termite/gravel costs; Pine Telephone fiber reaches 94%.

Exit honesty

DOM 121, sale-to-list 96–97% (a $530–560K ask to $500K is plausible; a $575K+ ask usually isn't), prices −15–25% off peak, cap ~5.3% on the one real NOI disclosure. LTR exit is dead; resale-to-the-next-DFW-STR-buyer is the only path. buildingPct 0.83 (real parcels) makes cost seg the strongest of any market screened: ~$38.4K Year-1 benefit at $500K.
