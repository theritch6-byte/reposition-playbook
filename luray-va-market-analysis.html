⚖ Regulatory & Legal
Page County — Amendment Pending

Public hearing September 21, 2026. Creates an owner-occupied short-term tourist rental class, rewrites the Residential district to owner-occupied-only, and adds special-use-permit lanes in W-C, A-1 and R — the districts where STR is by-right today. The debate is framed around the fact that over 50% of Page County STRs are owned from outside the county. Grandfathering is unadopted and unconfirmed. The full text is office-only. Page County Planning: (540) 743-1324.

Jurisdiction Table
  • Page County (unincorporated) — BY-RIGHT in W-C, A-1, R, C-1 (§125-30.21). Administrative only: business licence, management plan, life-safety inspection. No hearing. At risk Sept 21.
  • Town of Luray — SUP required for lodging houses (App. A, Art. V §519, Ord. 9-9-2024). Hard cap: 3 bedrooms / 6 overnight guests.
  • Town of Stanley — SPLIT: SUP in MDR/HDR residential; by-right in HC and TC commercial.
  • Town of Shenandoah — zoning text is a DATA GAP, but posture is openly pro-STR; the town markets ~19 vacation rentals on its own website.
  • Warren County (unincorporated) — CUP required always, in A, R-1, RR only. $1,750 fee, ~3-month process, 3 of 15 applications denied. A Warren CUP transfers with the property, updated administratively at no fee.
  • Town of Front Royal — SUP required in all districts. $1,200, Council approval, pre-issuance inspection.
  • Rappahannock County — effectively closed. The Zoning Administrator may not accept an application until the owner has held the parcel two years, plus 10/20-acre minimums.
POA / HOA Exposure

Multiple POA-governed communities sit inside the buyable inventory — Rocky Top Retreat (HOA $44/mo, private community with exclusive river access), 755 Wildwood (410-acre private wildlife community), Shenandoah Shores, Shenandoah Forest, Shenandoah Gap, White Pines, River Oak, Egypt Bend Estates. No recorded CC&Rs have been read for any parcel. Required clearance is a written statement from the association itself plus current recorded CC&Rs — never accept "it's grandfathered," which the prior market run proved does not transfer to a buyer.

Taxes

Page County transient occupancy tax is 10% — not the 5% widely republished. Virginia assesses at 100% of fair market value with rates per $100. Page moved to $0.52–0.54/$100 after a ~60% reassessment; Warren is $0.479/$100. Effective rate lands at ~0.60% of purchase price — materially gentler than Ohio's 1.47% post-sale reset. Check land-use (agricultural/forestal) deferral on any acreage parcel: Virginia rollback is typically 5 years of deferred tax plus interest on conversion.

📈 Demand & Market Fundamentals
Four Durable Drivers

Shenandoah National Park — ~1.7M recreation visits in 2024, third consecutive annual rise, permanent federal land (Skyline Drive stayed open through the October 2025 shutdown). Luray Caverns — ~500K visitors, indoors at a constant 54°F, a genuine weatherproof counter-seasonal hedge. Shenandoah River — outfitter in its 56th season. George Washington National Forest + 101 miles of the Appalachian Trail. Massanutten and Bryce add ski-season winter demand. Roughly 10M people in the DC/Baltimore feeder inside two hours.

Seasonality — the foliage fear is overstated

October is 14.3% of annual gross (1.71× a flat month), not the ~24% the park's own visitation implies — the foliage spike is largely day-trippers, not overnight guests. Luray is a three-peak market: October, August and May. No single-extreme-event overlay applies; foliage recurs permanently, peaks across 3+ weeks by elevation, and is weather-shiftable rather than cancellable.

Three dead months — and one is a surprise

January, February and July. July holds the year's highest ADR ($295) and still will not fill. February is the true trough at $2,419 revenue / 31.3% occupancy / $253 ADR. Skyline Drive closes 4–5+ times per winter for freezing rain and ice, for days at a time — an estimated 10–20 closed days a winter, weekend-weighted. That is the mechanism behind February's occupancy.

Revenue floor — knife-edge fail

The bar is $2,006/month (70% of P&I on $400,000 at 7.75%). Raw February revenue of $2,419 passes, but AirROI's monthly series over-sums against its own stated annual total; scaled correctly February lands at $1,998 — short by $8. An alternate dataset gives $2,141 (pass). The result straddles zero, so it is treated as a fail. A hot tub is a precondition, not an upgrade — every outdoor driver is offline December–March, so the cabin itself is the trip.

🏠 Competitive & Revenue
The carry-forward figures were wrong

ADR is $278–$295 (centre ~$285) across three independent pulls — the August screen's $225.81 is refuted, roughly 21% below the lowest independent figure. Supply growth is +17.9% to +18.4%, not the +1.9% carried forward — a tenfold error. Market average revenue is $37,512, occupancy 39.6–46%, RevPAR $117, 388 Airbnb listings / 602 all-platform (clears the small-market threshold by only 0.3%).

The "$127K–$135K top tier" is real Luray inventory — but it is the top ~2–4%, not the top decile

Luray's top-decile threshold is $87,096. The $127K–$135K figures name real Luray listings — the Aug-19 screen lists Blue Ridge Retreat 2 at $135K, Horizon at $128K and Blue Ridge Mini Lux Retreat at $127K — and AirROI independently verifies Horizon at $151,259. They are the top ~2–4% of the market, not the threshold. Both numbers are true; they measure different things. Six-figure Luray revenue demonstrably exists — it is simply not for sale under $600,000. Correct ladder for Luray: median $36,144 · top 25% $60,372 · top 10% $87,096. Genuine market ceiling: Golden Ridge Manor (5BR) $176,680 · Horizon (2BR) $151,259 · 3 Luxe SNP Cabins $257,875 (a three-door compound — treat as ~$86K/door).

Luray town is the WEAKEST of the three Page County sub-markets

Stanley ($53,542 avg · $348 ADR · top decile $125,532 · revenue +8.7%) and Shenandoah town ($54,833 · $373 ADR · top decile $121,548 · +8.6%) each out-earn Luray town ($37,512 · $289 ADR · +2.5%) by roughly $16,000/yr, or +43%. The shopping geofence should be Stanley first, Shenandoah town second, Luray town third. Front Royal is disqualified (+47.9% supply against −8.3% revenue); Basye/Bryce is disqualified (lowest ADR $238, RevPAR $90, −3.6% revenue).

Short-stay weekend market

ALOS is 2.7–2.9 nights — Friday to Sunday is the whole business. That means 50 turns a year at market occupancy and 82–88 at top-decile occupancy. At $150 a turn a top-decile Stanley property runs ~$13,200/yr in cleaning, roughly 10% of gross and the largest variable cost after the mortgage. Model cleaning per turn, never as a flat percentage of revenue. Dual-platform listing (Airbnb + VRBO) is the market standard — ~36% of supply is VRBO-visible.

🔧 Operations & Exit
There is no self-management discount for a remote owner

opExpRatio_remote = 0.819 against opExpRatio_with_pm = 0.810 at a $42,000 reference. For a Florida-based owner the 0.590 self-manage lane does not exist — a paid local co-host and the uncovered linen/deep-clean drag are mandatory, and they cost essentially what full property management costs.

miscSupplies = $17,100

Rebuilt from real local vendor pricing (band $9,670 low / $17,130 mid / $28,850 high). The $4,000 default was 4.3× too low — the fourth consecutive market where it failed. Drivers: propane, septic pumping and AOSS service contract, well treatment and testing, hot-tub chemicals/filters/cover cycle, winter freeze protection, gravel drive and snow/ice removal on Blue Ridge grades, tree work, rural trash haul, and mountain-hollow internet (Starlink).

Good news: management is normal here

Full-service PM runs a normal 20–30% (GoSummer from 20%, RedAwning ~25%), nothing like Hocking Hills' 40–50%. The cleaner ecosystem is abundant. Named local operators: Allstar Lodging and KBR Co-Hosting, both on E Main St, Luray.

Septic caps legal headcount

Virginia sizes onsite sewage at 75 gpd/person with two persons per bedroom = 150 gpd per bedroom (12VAC5-610-670, Table 5.1). That makes 2BR = 4 guests, 3BR = 6 guests, 4BR = 8 guests. A 3BR-certified cabin is legally a six-guest, median-ADR asset with roughly a $38,000 gross ceiling. Upgrading 3BR→4BR costs $12–30K conventional or $20–55K alternative, plus a 50–100% reserve drainfield many riverfront lots cannot supply. Regulator: VDH Lord Fairfax Health District (Page and Warren).

Insurance and flood

Virginia's FAIR plan (VPIA) does write absentee, non-owner-occupied dwellings — unlike Ohio's — but excludes liability on them and excludes vacancy. It is a property-only lifeboat, not a usable STR backstop. Flood decision rule: buy river ACCESS, never river FOOTPRINT. Dwelling and septic drainfield/reserve entirely in Zone X with the lot extending to the water. Automatic walk-away if the floor is at or below BFE, the drainfield sits in the SFHA, access crosses the SFHA, there is no Elevation Certificate, or flood quotes exceed $3,500/yr. A mortgage forces flood insurance inside a Special Flood Hazard Area.

No exit cushion

The long-term-rental fallback fails outright: rent-to-price is 0.32%, roughly $20,000/yr short of debt service. Resale is the only exit and it is thin — rural Page County has a narrow buyer pool and soft days-on-market (367 Riverview 130 DOM, 107 Highfield 148 DOM). buildingPct from real assessor parcels: small-lot pre-renovation 0.86, acreage 0.83, renovated turnkey 0.94 (Page + Warren via Vision Government Solutions, 2026). Acreage and construction age drive the split — not water proximity.
